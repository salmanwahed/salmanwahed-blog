About

Hello, I'm Salman M Wahed.

A software engineer with over 12 years of experience, now working on agentic and generative AI — agent workflows built with LangChain and LangGraph, retrieval pipelines, and LLMs wired into real production services rather than demos.

The foundation under that is backend engineering in Python and Java: Django, FastAPI and Spring Boot, and the APIs and data models that hold a system together. I build the front end too, with React, and ship mobile apps with Kotlin Multiplatform.

Over the years I've led teams, architected scalable systems, and worked on high-impact products — including a FinTech app used by millions. I graduated from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology with a BSc (Engg.) in Computer Science & Engineering.

Outside of coding I enjoy swimming, cycling, and solving puzzles. I also love good sci-fi and am always up for a conversation about futuristic worlds — or just something interesting. If you have an idea you're excited about, reach out.
